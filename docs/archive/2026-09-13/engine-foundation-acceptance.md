## What is delivered

- Physical materials with spectral dispersion/absorption/scattering, unit-aware inputs and evidence provenance; scalar spectral, isotropic polarized and explicit uniaxial transport modes with capability admission.
- Procedural cut programs, analytic shapes, polygon lofts and general boundaries, with physical scale, validated topology, continuous convex rounding, workmanship and cleavage geometry.
- Named seeded condition presets that realize detached, inspectable specimens. The quartz study demonstrates reference, softened polish, cut tolerance, localized cloud and foreign crystals. Labels select physical states rather than acting as hidden shader effects.
- Low-noise volume reconstruction preserving sharp transport contributions, with independent/raw reference comparisons and practical render evidence. No universal error-free or noise-free claim.
- Optional separately cached game styling; retained unstyled prints permit headless restyling without rebaking optics.
- Explicit asset batches for quality/lighting/animation variants. Delivery names do not change physics; aliases reuse frames and lighting variants share geometry. No implicit rotation-by-board-cell Cartesian product.
- Frozen standalone Godot worker bundles, resume/sharding/transfer/cache maintenance, compressed bounded atlas pages, selective runtime loading and a rebuilt game asset pack.

## Explicit limits

The foundation is extensible, not a universal calibrated mineral simulator. Most catalog appearance parameters remain authored. Natural grade certification and calibrated growth/wear histories are not claimed. Fracture grading remains disabled after unsatisfactory morphology; specialized biaxial, anisotropic silk, fluorescence and structural-color transport are outside the validated modes. Rough/scattering crystal transport has capability restrictions. Actual Linux/render-farm/network-filesystem deployment remains unvalidated; portable independent Windows workers are tested.

These limits are preserved in contracts and evidence rather than filled with random visible marks. Further specialized physics, specimen calibration and finished game aesthetics are distinct future work. The baseline requested for procedural physical asset generation, condition authoring, styling separation and practical delivery is implemented and verified within these declared capabilities.
