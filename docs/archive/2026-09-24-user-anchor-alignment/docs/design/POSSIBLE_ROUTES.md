# Possible routes beyond the current prototype

Status: optional design space, consolidated 2026-09-24. Nothing here is an
implementation queue or a prerequisite for P4/P5. Read only when a task concerns
future direction. Detailed historical examples remain in the
[archived content proposal](../archive/2026-09-24-documentation-reset/docs/CONTENT_SYSTEMS.md).

## What to keep from the broader concept

The collection is the build: one active definition per tier, with supply modeled
separately. Mineral families suggest a coherent vocabulary of reactions; rooms
give those reactions useful spatial problems. Content should produce different
decisions without multiplying simultaneous match classes or obscuring identity.

| Route | Experience worth investigating | Smallest useful experiment | Main risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| More expressive builds | Rewards change setup and resolution choices | One Garnet consumption or Tourmaline assist signature with a paired room fixture | Adding a perk whose only effect is a larger number |
| More varied rooms | Familiar pieces solve different spatial problems | One asymmetric layout or one seal/dust rule before combining hazards | Unreachable objectives, unreadable layers, or tedious waiting |
| Transport and routing | Movement paths become intentional tactics | One authored gravity/portal room with explicit path presentation | Treating visual travel as rule authority or inventing matching adjacency |
| Lapidary and collection craft | Choosing specimens/cuts changes collection expression | A few compatible cosmetic cuts, or one explicitly mechanical technique | Confusing appearance, mineral identity and ability |
| Curated grade variants | A material can occupy a different authored appraisal band | One complete roster exchange with before/after and carry preview | A hidden second score or automatic grade-to-optics mapping |
| Special materials and pieces | Pattern, opacity, inlays or catalysts broaden the fantasy | One named piece role with a clear cost, limit and material capability study | Assuming the current renderer supports every desired phenomenon |
| Longer expeditions and progression | Build identity develops across more choices | The former six-room slice after the initial prototype supports it | Expanding before pacing and choice quality are understood |

These directions can compete for attention. Select one because it answers an
observed need, not because an old table listed it. The six-room P6 idea remains
a possible next scope, not part of finishing the present prototype.

## Design distinctions worth preserving

- Tier is one game appraisal/progression axis, not a universal mineral price.
  Grade metadata and physical specimen inputs are separate; a label cannot
  establish optical appearance or gemological value.
- A family is curated identity. A cross-family affinity, a gem-specific
  signature, a collection technique and an expedition setting are different
  possible design tools; none needs an unused framework today.
- A cosmetic cut may alter facets within an admitted readable tier envelope.
  A mechanical technique needs an explicit ability and rules identity. Neither
  silently changes the other's meaning.
- Opaque or organic material is not automatically low tier. A catalyst or
  masterwork is an exceptional role, not an obligatory T0/T9 extension of the ladder.
- Topology, objective, hazard pressure and rewards can vary independently, but
  their combined difficulty and readability must be evaluated together.

## Inspiration, not feature obligations

The earlier design drew on Merge Maestro for build expression through merging,
Treasure Hunt for accumulating value in surviving pieces, Slay the Spire for
reward/route consequences, Balatro for expressive combinations from a small
grammar, and match-3 objective modes for spatial goals. These are selected design
lessons, not promises to copy those games, their current rules or their scope.
New visual/audio references belong in [user anchors](USER_ANCHORS.md).

## Admission of a future experiment

Name the player decision it changes, its useful setup and limitation, its
required events/targets/budgets, and the room that makes it matter. Check actual
asset capabilities and accessible alternatives. Specify replay/save implications
when behavior changes. Build one complete example before generalizing.

An attractive new specimen can be cosmetic content. Do not invent a mechanical
reward solely to justify producing its art. Avoid a product of every gem, tier,
grade, cut, light, condition and effect; author the content the experience uses.
